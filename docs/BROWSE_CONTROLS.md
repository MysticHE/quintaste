# Browse controls and menu footer

Owner direction, 17 October 2026: keep Cook, Social People and Market browse controls within two rows, with consistent type, icon treatment and filter grouping. This replaces the earlier separate Cook category/actions/sort rows and large Market category circles. Events retains the explicit event-type row introduced by the concurrent event-discovery release, with Local events selected by default.

`BrowseControls` shares a search row, 48-point controls, outlined Ionicons, Nunito labels and semantic palette colors. Secondary actions scroll horizontally on narrow screens or at larger text sizes; they never wrap into additional option rows. Cook has Category, My recipes and Shopping. Market has Category, Saved, Orders and My Shop, with an always-visible filter control. Social retains its three keyboard-operable segmented tabs, Inbox and contextual filters. Resume, observance, loading and error feedback remain separate functional states.

The category shortcut opens the filter screen/dialog. Categories and sorting now have explicit named options. Filter sections share small labelled headings, restrained separators and compact selection chips. Cook and Social persist immediately and provide Done; Market keeps a draft until Show results, discards it on close and resets category with the other refinements. Saved-only remains an independent Market browse toggle. Independent dietary requirements and ingredient exclusions use checkboxes, while category, sort, duration, Social profile classification/time/group size and collection method remain exclusive. Social All diets includes both supported profile classifications. Market diets match all selected declarations; an ingredient exclusion checks both Contains and May contain. No filtering API or saved-data schema changed.

Cook's Show All Recipes also clears the search text. Market and Cook badges include a selected category. Ingredient controls expose checkbox semantics. Shared icon controls use muted outlines when inactive and the semantic accent/soft fill when selected; keyboard focus remains visible.

The menu footer now contains one source link labelled Last updated followed by the latest recorded source date in Singapore time. The user explicitly supersedes the earlier long Sources & availability disclosure: do not restore repeated source notices, charges paragraphs or duplicated links. Source data is retained unchanged. The date is the stored source check date, never a claim of live stock or merchant confirmation. Dish prices, unavailable-price states, pagination and caching are unchanged. Previous/next are always-present, labelled 48-point chevrons beside the dish range, disabled at the first/last page; a search/category change resets pagination.

Build and verification evidence is local under `.local/browse-controls/`. This is a UI-only delta from `20261017-event-discovery2`, including all earlier `review-hardening1` protections; preserve all existing backend and edge protections, additive migrations, editorial management and retained local test accounts. The first browse-controls1 build was never deployed: the live-base guard stopped it after event-discovery2 appeared, and the final build rebased onto that exact release. Browser fixtures intercept APIs and do not create real accounts or production content. Native transport verification is separate from physical-device appearance and accessibility acceptance.
