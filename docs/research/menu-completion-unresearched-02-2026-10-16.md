- Green Harmony’s current operator profile names the vegetarian business at exact 443 AngMoKioAve10 #01-1221. Its current catalogue has 30 explicit vegetarian dietary attributes and a named ovo-lacto AyamGepuk. Fifteen generic contradictory animal-description rows remain held individually. August2026 launch/promotion prices from a third-party operator-post mirror were used for discovery/scope only, never as current delivery prices.
- Xie Bi An’s street and unit are confirmed by its current ordering page: 824 TampinesStreet81 #01-38. The provider prints postal520849, while the independently corroborated directory address is520824. App address proposal omits the postal code pending resolution. Its WantonNoodle row mentions shrimp dumplings generically and remains held.
- Ann Healthy’s 43 food rows remain under an entire-venue hold: a Vegetarian venue title conflicts with descriptions of pork ribs, prawn/pork stock and meats, and no separate current operator whole-venue source resolved this. This is uncertainty, not an assertion of actual animal-food service.
- AMK407 has a September2026 independent entry explicitly describing a coffee shop serving meat with vegan options; a separate group post describes a vegetarian stall. The exact venue/stall scope remains unresolved, so nothing is proposed for publication.
- 3WAY’s current listing labels 7 of 8 foods vegetarian, including MeatBalls. The brownie has no explicit label. Independent sources call the cafe fully vegan, but current operator wellness sources do not expressly establish whole-cafe food scope. All 8 foods stay held. The wellness #B2-03 address is not substituted for cafe #B2-18/19/20/21; directory #B2-22 remains unconfirmed.
- Green Delights has strong current operator vegetarian venue proof. Its linked cover is marked July2023 and gives no named dishes/prices. Its readable CNY images expressly apply 9February–3March2026; the 7vegetables offer is specifically23February2026. All 5 offers are historical holds with current price unknown; three meal sets retain every printed component and 4/6/10-person portions without counting components as standalone dishes.
- Merchant profile foods are limited to explicitly named specialities. Generic product types such as bread, cake and pastry were not counted. Independent recommendation names were not turned into merchant menu rows. No dishes were inferred from photographs.
