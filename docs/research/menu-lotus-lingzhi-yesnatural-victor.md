# Lotus, LingZhi, Yes Natural and Victor Veggie menu research

Checked **15 October 2026**. Data: [`menu-lotus-lingzhi-yesnatural-victor.json`](../../data/menu-research/menu-lotus-lingzhi-yesnatural-victor.json). This is factual raw research for the wider 2,322-place collection. It does **not** establish merchant/community review, live stock, a complete current menu at every outlet, or completion of all 2,322 locations.

The packet contains **28 source records, four outlet identities, 14 source menus and 574 source-specific food records**: **415 base prices and 159 unpriced records**, with **31 portion/price options and 52 modifier groups**. The unpriced records comprise **156 set/lunch food choices or courses, two dated Victor Veggie dish labels and one discount-only promotion**. Repeated dishes across dine-in, delivery, set and historical sources remain separate evidence; these are not 574 unique dishes.

Of these records, **455 belong to identified outlet contexts**. The remaining **119 Lotus FoodLine records**—86 priced foods plus 33 unpriced set choices—remain **at brand scope** because its currently active menu and provider index do not publish an outlet address. Their prices are not assigned to Balestier or substituted for its Oddle prices.

Coverage is **nine source-covered menus, four partial menus and one unavailable menu**. `source-covered` means the accessible published source was collected; it does not certify current operation, stock, dietary status or a menu's effective date.

## Prices, sizes, choices and dates

- Both readable Oddle menus explicitly declare **SGD**. All food variants, modifier names/prices, minimum/maximum selection counts, status/preselection fields and nested option groups are retained. No direct `/api/` route was requested.
- Source `priceUnderMinimumSelection` values sometimes differ from option `price`. Both remain separately labelled without assuming billing semantics or calculating a total. Modifier prices are source option values, not standalone dish prices. Set-choice food records themselves have **no price**.
- Lotus's mini buffet retains **10/15/20-person source variants**, all food selections and its optional self-heating trays. Tray options are equipment within the source buffet, not dishes. Its nine-course family feast retains all nine food-selection/course contexts. No drink inclusion is inferred.
- Three Lotus Oddle food rows have nonzero item status codes: **Pan Fried Pumpkin w Shredded Taro**, **Crispy Enoki Mushroom** and **Taiwan Noodles in Thick Soup**. These source codes do not establish current stock or permanent closure. FoodLine independently labels **Lotus Trio** and **Wakame Salad with Wafu Dressing** `Not Available`; that source label is retained separately.
- Yes Natural party sets retain the source's **three-day preorder** condition, **choose five/choose seven** requirements and every named option. No per-choice standalone price is inferred from party-set source values.
- Yes Natural's official noodle image gives the page-wide price **8.80 for any noodle dish**. This explicit statement is applied to all ten printed noodle dishes. Its dry-fried/dry-mixed mee sua and Hor Fun/Bee Hoon wording is preserved without inventing surcharges.
- The soup image explicitly permits eight add-ons for **S1–S7**, excluding **S8–S10**. Those eight options and printed amounts are attached only to the affected seven soups. Selection limits are not printed and are not invented.
- Yes Natural's **Zen Garden Broth** prints **9.80 / 26.80** with codes **S8/S9**, but no adjacent portion labels. Both amounts are retained as unlabelled price options. Its **Delightful Daily Specialty Soup** is limited to **Monday, Wednesday and Friday**, excluding public holidays and the first/fifteenth lunar days.
- All seven Yes Natural lunch sets explicitly include a selected **Cool Sips drink**. Package rows and prices are excluded. Their named food portions remain unpriced, scoped to **11:00–15:00**, with no invented day restriction or copied à la carte food price.
- LingZhi's à la carte and set PDFs state **10% service charge plus prevailing government tax**. The promotion repeats that condition. Yes Natural's images state **service charge and GST**, without printed rates. The packet preserves those terms; it does not calculate taxes or totals.
- LingZhi, Yes Natural image menus, FoodLine and the Victor interview use plain `$` or numeric amounts without an explicit ISO currency declaration in those sources. Their price currencies remain **unknown**, even though the restaurant country is Singapore. Oddle's explicit SGD declaration is not transferred to another source.
- FoodLine's **120 minimum spend excludes GST and delivery**. This is a menu-level minimum, not a dish price. The source's public item prices remain unchanged; no checkout or final bill is calculated.
- Yes Natural's public ordering header still contains **17 February 2026 CNY-menu instructions** and **18–21 February 2026 closure dates**. These are historical source notices and do not imply the restaurant is closed in October.
- Victor's interview reports **10 sticks for $12** for the owner's mock chicken satay and **10 sticks for $15** for mock mutton satay; **$2.50** for vegetarian otah has no explicit unit quantity. These are dated reported prices, not current merchant-confirmed offers. The two additional Chinese food labels remain unpriced, with no separate rice/noodle prices invented.
- Dish/variant names containing words such as “Vegan” remain source names only. No ingredient certification, vegan/allium status, Google identity, customer review or app review badge is inferred.
