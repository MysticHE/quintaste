# Menu publication review

Review scope: all 183 branch research records in the 15 October 2026 collection index. Review performed on 16 October 2026 SGT; original captures generally date to 15 October. This is a source-eligibility review for factual names, prices, source links and observed dates. It is not a whole-directory completion finding, a stock guarantee, or ingredient certification.

The initial manifest saved 82 additional branches and 106 source-specific menus. Five additional reviewed branches and two Yes Natural sections are supplied in the machine-readable appendix because the parent compiler owns fingerprint writes during integration. With those applied: 87 additional branch records, 116 selected branch/menu associations and 8392 eligible source rows before compiler normalization. Shared branch menus and overlapping sources are counted per branch; these are not unique dishes.

Record accounting: 87 reviewed additions, 5 research records overlapping existing pilot data, 79 other research records deferred, 11 previously published pilot records retained, and one pilot record without a readable menu = 183 records. The research portion comprises 171 records across 46 indexed data files. The pre-existing pilot approval remains a separate manifest.

## Publication boundaries

- No unreviewed Google place ID, Google address substitution or exact identity assignment is authorized. Google matching remains independently reviewed; unmatched venues keep null IDs.
- Source-covered means all named foods visible in the inspected source. Partial menus are eligible only with their specific gaps. An undated linked menu is an observed publication, not proof that every food or price is current today.
- Current branch source identity, a valid independent six-digit Singapore postal code and menu-to-branch relationship are required. Brand-only menus are withheld except the Ananda home-delivery source expressly identifying self-collection at 95 Syed Alwi Road.
- Existing menu/item holds, expired dated offers, future collection holds, inactive/unresolved leads, unreadable menus, retail goods, beverage-inclusive package prices and copied artwork remain excluded. Makan Mandai is a central kitchen and is excluded from this consumer venue list.
- Explicitly source-unavailable rows in selected menus are excluded from new publication. Nonzero Oddle product/category codes are treated conservatively as publication exclusions without guessing that they mean permanent closure.
- Set components stay unpriced; package prices are never distributed across courses. Minimum order, preorder, weekday, portion, packing, tax and unknown-currency facts must survive projection. Listing prices are not fallback canonical prices when the item has a documented conflict.
