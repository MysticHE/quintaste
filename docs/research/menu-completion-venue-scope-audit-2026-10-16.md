# Published venue scope audit — 16 October 2026

All **119 selected published branches are accounted for** across two reproducible units: the original 113-branch freeze and a separate six-branch delta. Results: **107 vegetarian-confirmed, 11 vegan-confirmed and one uncertain-hold**. No additional venue was proven to serve actual meat/fish. Kueh Ho Jiak and The Living Cafe were already excluded by root before this freeze and are outside these counts.

This audits whole-venue eligibility, not individual vegetarian dishes at mixed venues. The practical standard accepts explicit operator vegetarian/vegan positioning, or a merchant-managed venue explicitly named Vegetarian with a coherent menu and no unresolved actual meat/fish contradiction. Eggs, dairy and honey remain vegetarian. Source claims do not establish independent ingredient/allergen certification, current stock, operating status or a complete/current menu.

## Important distinctions

- **Metta Cafe:** the old 2014/15 annual report described mixed food. The current programme page now links a later [Metta Cafe lunch menu](https://www.metta.org.sg/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Cafe-New-Menu-1-Aug-1024x724.png), visually inspected in this audit. It has an explicit Vegetarian logo, egg markers and coherent plant-based/mock-meat dishes. That later current-linked menu supports present vegetarian scope. The formal conversion date is unknown, and the historical contradiction remains recorded.
- **Merle & Co:** its [current homepage FAQ](https://merleandco.com/) states meat-free food and no animal/seafood products, and says it serves vegetarian/vegan foods. Dairy milk is offered, so the entire venue is classified vegetarian. Broad plant-based wording is not expanded into a vegan guarantee.
- **Tofu G:** the six exact current branch menus are coherent dessert-only food lists. Its [FAQ](https://www.tofug.sg/faq) says products contain no gelatin, pork, lard or alcohol. Selected gelatos are vegan-friendly; others contain dairy. Therefore vegetarian venue scope is supported, not whole-venue vegan scope. Root separately corrected toppings to one shared $2 add-on with 12 choices; this audit changes no food rows.
- **Teng Bespoke:** its frozen current merchant-managed ordering evidence explicitly names Vegetarian Japanese Dining and has a coherent vegetarian menu. Current direct retrieval gives a generic placeholder. Vegetarian venue identity is supported; current operation and menu availability remain unconfirmed and are not upgraded by this audit.
- **Sproutz Downtown:** only the exact [Downtown operator](https://www.sproutzdowntown.com/) and its vegetarian bentos are assessed. Other similarly named businesses are not conflated.
- **Original Greens:** the merchant ordering menu has vegan, vegan-optional and dairy labels over a coherent vegetarian catalogue. Its exact-address [booking profile](https://www.chope.co/singapore-restaurants/restaurant/original-greens) separately describes vegetarian/plant-based dining. It is not labelled wholly vegan.
- **OMG and Kind Kones:** HTTP 429 responses were respected. OMG's [official brand FAQ](https://omgdecadentdonuts.com/faq/) and [explicit Singapore affiliation](https://omgdecadentdonuts.com/locations/) support its vegan donut/topping business; its entire local beverage scope is not certified, so the outlet receives the conservative vegetarian verdict. Kind Kones relies on the same-session primary HTTP 200 evidence preserved in its canonical eligibility record, checked around 00:26 UTC; the audit's later 429 did not erase that evidence or justify a retry.

Grouped operator claims apply only where the frozen merchant name, exact store ID or official locator explicitly establishes affiliation. The audit does not transfer vegetarian status to sibling mixed concepts, entire hotels, co-located merchants, similarly named outlets or other branches without that association.
