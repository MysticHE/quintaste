# Friendship, people-discovery and group-meetup apps: per-app benchmark inventory (researched 17 October 2026)

Scope: friendship and group-meetup products benchmarked for a people-first social dining feature (opt-in profiles, invitations to groups of 2/4/6, chat before meeting, organiser-posted gatherings). Romance features are noted only where they affect the friendship graph or safety design. Source-quality flags: **[competitor]** = written by a rival app; **[vendor]** = the company's own marketing/help copy; **[snippet]** = taken from a search-result summary, page not fetched in full.

## 1. Which apps are active in October 2026, and which have shut down or pivoted?

### Takeaway
Bumble's friendship product is now a separate free app called "BFF" (rebuilt on Geneva, Sept 2025), Geneva and IRL are dead, Pie has moved from an events list toward a community network (Aug 2026), and Partiful started charging money in 2026 through ticketing (June) and Partiful Plus (Oct). Timeleft, Meetup, Luma, 222 and Peanut are active. Hey! VINA looks inactive, but there is no official notice. Patook's current status is unverified, and no friendship app called "Clyde" could be found (Clyx is the likely intended app).

## 2. Profile composition, verification, and what is required before discovery

### Takeaway
Friendship apps that rely on 1:1 discovery (BFF, Peanut, Hey! VINA, Patook) use dating-style profiles: photos, prompts, interest tags or badges, life stage, and mandatory selfie verification. Curated group-dining apps (Timeleft, 222) hide profiles almost entirely and rely on a private questionnaire plus a real name and photo. Event apps (Partiful, Luma, Meetup) need only a phone number or email, and are now adding richer profiles and previews of who is attending.

### Cited Findings
**Bumble BFF (2025–26 app)**
- Profile tools: bios, custom Interest Tags and Photo Prompts. "Every match passes selfie verification." Rated 18+ — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/1478573199).
- Verification is required of all members before they can use the app. Users choose which genders they want to match with, and matching historically defaulted to same-gender — [NexSpark [competitor]](https://nexspark.org/journal/bumble-bff-review).
- Earlier Bumble For Friends profiles had:
  - profile prompts, with advice to answer specifically;
  - 3–4 photos recommended;
  - up to 3 milestone badges;
  - an education badge;
  - a university sticker after verifying a student email.
  - Sources: [Bumble profile tips [vendor]](https://bumble.com/bff/friendship/profile-tips); [Bumble college tips [vendor][snippet]](https://bumble.com/the-buzz/bumble-for-friends-college-tips).
- Photo verification: the user copies one of about 100 random poses in a selfie, and a human moderator reviews it. It is free and can be retried — [Bumble Buzz [vendor][snippet]](https://bumble.com/the-buzz/request-verification).
- Users had complained about selfie-verification glitches and superficial 1:1 connections before the revamp — [The Everygirl [snippet]](https://theeverygirl.com/bumble-for-friends-app/).

**Timeleft**
- Onboarding is a short assessment covering personality, social energy, neighbourhood, language, dietary needs and budget. Changing the profile updates future matches — [Timeleft guide, 18 Sep 2026 [vendor]](https://timeleft.com/blog/how-does-timeleft-work/).
- Members must give their real legal name and a clear, unfiltered photo, and may be asked for ID. Minimum age is 18, or 21+ in the UAE — [Timeleft](https://timeleft.com/blog/how-does-timeleft-work/).
- The personality test asks how much family, spirituality, challenge and humour matter to the user — [Timeleft algorithm post [vendor][snippet]](https://timeleft.com/post/timeleft-algorithm-the-maestro-of-your-dinners/).
- Matching uses personality, interests and age, "not photos or bios" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6466442949).
- Before the dinner, guests learn only minor details about each other, such as occupation and zodiac sign, the night before — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/).

**222**
- 2022: sign-up through a Typeform asking name, email and date of birth, then about 30 Myers-Briggs-style questions. Topics included movies, music, politics, religion, income, sexual orientation, college major, diet and ZIP code. The algorithm placed users into 16 personality categories. Identity was verified mainly through payment information, and ages were limited to 18–27 — [TechCrunch, 14 Nov 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/14/2440702/).
- 2026: reviewers describe the intake form as "like taking a psychology test". 222 claims matching improves after about 5 events — [NexSpark [competitor]](https://nexspark.org/journal/222-review).

**Pie**
- 2024 profile: phone-number sign-up, interests picked from a prewritten list, and a photo. Optional fields: Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, city, bio — [Business Insider via Yahoo, Nov 2024](https://tech.yahoo.com/apps/articles/tried-pie-social-million-dollar-130801055.html).
- 2026: a "favorites" feature lets users list interests on their public profile — [TechCrunch, 25 Aug 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/andy-dunns-startup-pie-becomes-less-of-an-events-app-and-more-of-a-social-network/).

**Hey! VINA (historic)**
- Profile fields: "Current Status" bio, quiz results, favourite activities, life stage, and age/location preferences.
- Linking Facebook validated gender; Instagram sign-in was added in Aug 2018.
- Source: [Hey! VINA FAQ](https://www.heyvina.com/faq).

**Peanut**
- "All profiles on Peanut are checked with selfie verification."
- Incognito Mode for anonymous questions, and a "zero tolerance" abuse policy.
- Source: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/peanut-find-mom-friends/id1178656034).

**Patook (2016–17)**
- Users complete a profile and questionnaire and rank which traits matter to them — [VML [snippet]](https://vml.com/insight/platonic-dating-apps).
- An NLP "flirt detector" blocks romantic messages before delivery and auto-bans users. It was trained on pickup lines and creepy messages and also uses behavioural signals: who a user messages, how often, and whether messages are copy-pasted. In beta, 5% of users were banned before their first message was delivered — [Engadget, Jun 2017](https://www.engadget.com/2017-06-27-tinder-for-friends-uses-ai-to-block-flirty-messages.html); [GeekWire, 2016](https://www.geekwire.com/2016/patook-llc/).

**Meetup**
- Profiles were redesigned in 2023, with all information optional — [Meetup blog [vendor]](https://www.meetup.com/blog/new-and-improved-member-profiles/).
- 2026 roadmap: "richer member profiles with more details and additional photos" — [Meetup roadmap](https://www.meetup.com/blog/2026-meetup-roadmap/).
- Event pages are to show a gender and age breakdown of people who signed up ("coming soon", Dec 2025) — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/mobile/meetups-new-mobile-app-is-designed-to-make-it-easier-to-meet-people-irl-150000920.html).
- The attendee list shows attendees' interests, shared interests, and what members are looking for (redesigned Aug 2023) — [Meetup release notes](https://www.meetup.com/blog/meetup-product-release-notes/).

**Partiful**
- Guests can RSVP without installing the app, but need a verified phone number — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partiful).
- Partiful Plus (Oct 2026) adds "expanded profile details and accessories" for how users appear on guest lists — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/partiful-launches-partiful-plus-subscription-with-new-customization-features-302907923.html).

**Synchrony (2026)**
- Two-step verification: both the user and a trusted person verify details and photo ID — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/).

**Yubo**
- Requires a live face photo for AI age estimation. If the estimate conflicts with the stated age, further ID steps follow. Age-gated communities keep teens and adults apart, and about 67k accounts a month were suspended over age discrepancies (2022 data) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/25/gen-z-social-app-yubo-rolls-out-age-estimating-technology-to-better-identify-minors-using-its-service); [Yubo newsroom](https://yubo.live/newsroom/yubo-to-become-first-major-social-media-platform-to-age-verify-100-of-its-users).

### Inferences
- Selfie or photo verification is the baseline trust signal for any app where strangers browse each other. Curated dinner apps instead rely on real-name rules, payment identity and post-event reporting.
- Two design patterns:
  - **Questionnaire-first** (Timeleft, 222, Pie circles): private answers drive grouping, and visible profile detail is minimal.
  - **Profile-first** (BFF, Peanut): public prompts and interest tags drive 1:1 choice.
- A people-first dining feature with self-chosen invitations is closer to the profile-first pattern, so verification and anti-romance guardrails like Patook's become more important.
- "Who's going" previews are a growing product lever: Meetup's age/gender breakdown and interest-annotated attendee list, and Partiful's paid "Peek".

### Gaps
- No official 2026 Bumble help-centre page on BFF profile fields beyond the App Store copy was retrieved.
- Whether Timeleft enforces ID checks routinely is unknown; it says only "may be requested".
- Location granularity per app (exact distance vs neighbourhood) is documented only for Hey! VINA (~50 miles) and Partiful Peek (neighbourhood only).

## 3. Discovery and matching: swipe vs browse vs curated algorithmic groups

### Takeaway
There are three discovery models:
- **Swipe for 1:1 matches:** BFF, Peanut, Hey! VINA, Patook, Wyzr.
- **Browse groups and events:** Meetup, BFF Groups, Pie, Clyx, Luma calendars, Facebook Local.
- **Algorithmically curated small tables:** Timeleft (4–6), Pie circles (6), 222 (vetted selection), Les Amís (Monday batch matches).

Group sizes of 4–6 dominate the curated dining products.

### Cited Findings
**Timeleft**
- Typically 4–6 per table, with a mix of ages and backgrounds but no guaranteed age range. Matching uses quiz answers, age band, language and preferences, and a preview of the group appears in-app before arrival — [Timeleft](https://timeleft.com/blog/how-does-timeleft-work/).
- TechCrunch says each user is "matched with four others" and that matching considers age, gender and personality — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/).
- App Store copy: "No swiping. No planning. Just show up." — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6466442949).

**Pie**
- In Nov 2024 Dunn said the original **1:1 matching quiz was dropped because friendships form in group settings**. Invitations run through friends-of-friends, like LinkedIn's first- and second-degree connections — [TechCrunch, 19 Nov 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/19/irl-social-app-pie-is-coming-to-sf-to-make-you-less-lonely).
- By 2025–26, each person who RSVPs to a Pie event completes a short AI personality assessment and is sorted into a **group of six** with a pre-event group chat. Available in Austin, Chicago and San Francisco — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/).
- The quiz covered topics such as stability, astrology, voting and personal habits (feature launched Mar 2025) — [Social Discovery Insights [snippet]](https://www.socialdiscoveryinsights.com/2025/03/05/social-app-pie-introduces-ai-powered-friendship-matchmaking/).
- Dec 2025: the activity feed changed to show **which of your connections are attending events**, not just which events exist — [TechCrunch, 25 Aug 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/andy-dunns-startup-pie-becomes-less-of-an-events-app-and-more-of-a-social-network/).

**222**
- Selection-based: the algorithm builds the group and plans the venue and itinerary. People not selected can join post-event mingling (2022) — [TechCrunch 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/14/2440702/).
- In 2026, selected participants are notified on the day of the event — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/).
- Reviewers report repeated "high demand" declines — [NexSpark [competitor]](https://nexspark.org/journal/222-review).

**Bumble BFF**
- Keeps 1:1 swipe matching plus a Groups tab. Groups range "from small groups to thousands of members" — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/18/bumble-bffs-revamped-app-is-here-focusing-on-friend-groups-and-community-building).
- Groups discovery covers "local and national groups" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/1478573199).

**Meetup**
- Discovery by topic and group. "Personalized event recommendations" shipped in 2025 — [Meetup roadmap](https://www.meetup.com/blog/2026-meetup-roadmap/).
- Organizers can tag events with up to 5 topics — [Meetup [snippet]](https://www.meetup.com/blog/16-recent-meetup-improvements-you-might-have-missed/).

**Hey! VINA (historic)**
- Swipe right or left; messaging only after a match.
- Open "Plans" are visible to users within about 50 miles, and a Discover tab lists open plans to RSVP to.
- Source: [Hey! VINA FAQ](https://www.heyvina.com/faq).

**Patook**
- Tinder-like swipe with an optional list view — [TechCrunch 2017](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/27/patook-wants-to-be-tinder-for-platonic-friendships).

**Peanut**
- "Swipe to meet local moms at every life stage" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/peanut-find-mom-friends/id1178656034).

**Les Amís**
- AI matches are released **every Monday**; users then chat and plan meetups later that week, e.g. pottery or book clubs — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/).

**Washed Up (LA, 2026)**
- Users create or join small-group "plans" tied to specific events, **request to join**, then chat with the group — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/).

**Clyx**
- Aggregates events from Ticketmaster and TikTok. Users can upload contacts to see which events friends are attending, and the app recommends people to connect with at those events — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/).

**Mmotion (NYC)**
- A map of active users; users join interest groups and message people nearby. Entry by application — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/).

**Facebook**
- The Local tab, tested Oct 2024 in 10 US cities, merges Marketplace, Groups and Events.
- The Explore tab has "For You" and "Nearby" feeds.
- Weekly and weekend event digest notifications.
- Source: [TechCrunch, 4 Oct 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/04/facebook-launches-a-gen-z-focused-redesign).

**Partiful**
- Graph of "Mutuals" (people you have attended events with), plus feeds of events friends are attending — [Global Dating Insights, 15 Jan 2026](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/partifuls-crush-tool-brings-dating-style-matching-to-event-app/).

### Inferences
- Pie dropped its 1:1 quiz in favour of group grouping, and Bumble moved from 1:1 swipes to groups. Both point the same way: **small-group formats beat 1:1 for friendship**, which supports a 2/4/6 model in which 4 and 6 are likely the strongest formats.
- Weekly batch cadences (Timeleft's Wednesday dinners, Les Amís' Monday matches) concentrate supply. That helps density in a cold market.
- Graph-based discovery ("connections attending", Mutuals) is the newest lever in the event apps.

### Gaps
- Distance and radius filter specifics for BFF and Meetup in 2026 were not documented in the sources retrieved.
- How Timeleft balances gender per table is undocumented; users complain about imbalance (see Q7).

## 4. Invitation and connection flow: request, accept, chat; group chat creation; icebreakers; how plans are confirmed

### Takeaway
Curated apps remove planning altogether: the system books the venue and reveals it the day before, then provides icebreakers and post-event "connect" requests. Peer apps use match-then-chat (BFF) or RSVP-then-chat (Pie, Partiful). The invite tools (Partiful, Luma, WhatsApp) supply the confirmation mechanics: date polls, RSVP yes/maybe/no, +1s, text blasts, reminders at fixed offsets, and calendar sync.

### Cited Findings
**Timeleft**
- Booking flow: choose a format (dinner, drinks, coffee or walk, depending on city), then a date, then confirm the subscription. Bookings close **13 hours** before the event — [Timeleft](https://timeleft.com/blog/how-does-timeleft-work/).
- Venue name and address are released the day before. The table number appears 1 hour before start. Dinners last about 3 hours — [Timeleft](https://timeleft.com/blog/how-does-timeleft-work/).
- The App Store copy says the venue is revealed on the day of the event — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6466442949). This contradicts the "day before" timing above; the difference may be by city.
- In-app icebreakers at every table; TechCrunch describes a conversation-starter game — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6466442949); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/).
- Lock-screen Live Activity countdown before the event — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6466442949).
- After dinner:
  - **Connect** sends connection requests to people met.
  - **Impressions** shows a summary of what others appreciated about you.
  - **Timeleft Repeat** helps reconnect.
  - Optional **Last Drinks** bar stop in select cities.
  - Sources: [Timeleft](https://timeleft.com/blog/how-does-timeleft-work/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6466442949).

## 5. Events and gatherings: creation, capacity, approval vs open join, cancellation, host tools, post-event feedback, no-shows

### Takeaway
The host-tool set has standardised:
- capacity caps with waitlists;
- per-ticket or per-event approval with screening questions;
- pending guests excluded from reminders;
- QR check-in;
- attendance and no-show flags;
- post-event feedback emails or ratings.

No-show handling splits two ways:
- **Financial penalty:** Timeleft charges 35% of the monthly fee.
- **Organizer-side flags:** Meetup lets organizers mark no-shows, and some groups remove members after 3. No platform penalty is documented.

### Cited Findings
**Luma**
- Approval is set **per ticket** and works on free or paid tickets. Registrants show as "Pending" until approved. Invited guests are auto-approved even when approval is on. Hosts see registration answers before approving or declining — [Luma Help](https://help.luma.com/p/setting-up-ticket-types); [Luma Help](https://help.luma.com/p/managing-your-guest-list).
- Waitlist: the host sets a capacity and turns on "Over-Capacity Waitlist". Waitlisted guests must be approved **manually** when spots open, so a cancellation does not auto-fill. Paid waitlisted guests' cards are authorised and charged only on approval — [Luma Help](https://help.luma.com/p/waitlist).
- After the event, Luma sends a feedback-collection email (customisable in the Blasts tab), and the host is emailed for each feedback response — [Luma Help](https://help.luma.com/p/notification-types).
- Calendars that members follow; paid memberships (monthly, yearly or one-time); QR check-in from the iOS/Android apps — [Luma Help](https://help.luma.com/p/luma-vs-meetup).
- Luma Plus adds a check-in manager role — [Party.pro [snippet]](https://party.pro/luma/).

**Meetup**
- **Event Check-In** (organizer app):
  - opens 1 hour before the event and closes 24 hours after;
  - swipe right to check in, left to mark absent; bulk check-in available;
  - a **no-show flag** (RSVPed yes, never came) is distinct from an "absence" (told the organizer in advance);
  - organizers see guest counts and whether this is someone's first event with the group;
  - some groups remove members after 3 no-shows; no platform penalty is described.
  - Source: [Meetup blog, modified Jan 2024](https://www.meetup.com/blog/new-to-meetup-the-event-check-in-feature-for-organizers/).

- Source: [Meetup roadmap](https://www.meetup.com/blog/2026-meetup-roadmap/).

## 6. Retention, engagement patterns and cold-start for new cities

### Takeaway
The best-documented cold-start playbook is Timeleft's:
- an MVP built on Typeform, WhatsApp and Stripe;
- paid ads instead of city ambassadors;
- a fixed weekly time slot (Wednesday dinners);
- later, retrenchment from 320+ to about 200 cities because thin markets failed. Its stated threshold is 50+ weekly participants per market.

Pie's levers were paying hosts per RSVP and showing friends' attendance in the feed. Meetup's 2025–26 levers are recommendations, calendar sync and reviews. Clyx seeds supply by aggregating external events.

### Cited Findings
**Timeleft**
- Timeline:
  - **May 2023:** 15-day test of weekly stranger dinners built with Typeform, a WhatsApp group and a Stripe link; first week €125.
  - **Dec 2023:** €20k MRR.
  - **Jan 2024:** first international city (Barcelona), launched via ads.
  - **2024:** grew to 320+ cities at about one a day, with **no local ambassadors or city managers**; €1M monthly revenue 16 months after launch.
  - **2025:** scaled back to 200+ (about 220) cities. Many small cities fizzled because density was too low, and **users got bored dining with the same small group every week**.
- Benchmark for a sustainable market: **50+ weekly participants**.
- Engagement rose from 1.4 to 2.2 experiences per member per month.
- About €18M ARR and about 150k monthly participants (Aug 2025).
- New formats (bar meetups, women-only dinners, running groups) went through three tests: friends and family, then a paid-marketing test, then in-app integration if acquisition cost worked.
- Source: [Tim Frin newsletter, 2025](https://timfrin.substack.com/p/inside-timelefts-journey-to-connecting).
- Fixed cadence:
  - Dinners every Wednesday ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6466442949)), at 7:00 p.m. ([TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/)).
  - Women-Only Dinners on Tuesdays at 7pm in 50+ cities.
  - Timeleft Run.
  - Timeleft Coffee on Saturdays.
  - Source: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6466442949).
- An earlier profile cited 25,000 people dining every Wednesday across 320+ cities — [Timeleft search summary of temy.co [snippet]](https://www.temy.co/breaking-bread-building-bonds-how-maxime-barbiers-timeleft-is-redefining-connections-in-cities-worldwide/).
- Barbier said the company should have focused on depth over breadth — [Neweconomies [snippet]](https://www.neweconomies.co/p/16m-net-arr-in-2-years).
- Retention features: weekly one-tap rebooking, inviting a connection to the next event, Repeat, Impressions and Connect — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6466442949); [Timeleft](https://timeleft.com/blog/how-does-timeleft-work/).

**Pie**
- A $1M Creator Fund pays hosts **$5–$10 per RSVP**. The top host series (Sunday Morning Club) reportedly had about a $100k run rate. Pie launched in Chicago with San Francisco as its second city, and had about 23k users in Oct 2024 — [TechCrunch, 19 Nov 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/19/irl-social-app-pie-is-coming-to-sf-to-make-you-less-lonely).
- After the Dec 2025 feed change to show connections attending events, time in app rose **from about 2 to about 10 minutes**. It has about 300k users, with most traction in Chicago, New York and Los Angeles.
- Nadya Okamoto, founder of the run club Zoomies, joined as co-founder and CMO, an example of recruiting community supply.
- Next on the roadmap: group chats and casual "seeking connection" posts without a set plan.
- Source: [TechCrunch, 25 Aug 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/andy-dunns-startup-pie-becomes-less-of-an-events-app-and-more-of-a-social-network/).
- Early density was thin: about one new event a day in Chicago at first, and most attendees had found events without Pie — [BI via Yahoo, Nov 2024](https://tech.yahoo.com/apps/articles/tried-pie-social-million-dollar-130801055.html).

**Meetup**
- 2025 shipped personalised recommendations, automatic Google Calendar events, public group reviews and a free "Meetup Starter" plan for first-time organizers. **75% of organizers started as members** — [Meetup roadmap](https://www.meetup.com/blog/2026-meetup-roadmap/).
- Meetup+ "priority event announcements" — "Say goodbye to event waitlists" — [Meetup blog](https://www.meetup.com/blog/introducing-member-learn-about-meetups-premium-member-subscription/).

**Bumble BFF**
- Migrated Geneva's existing groups wholesale into BFF, giving the new Groups tab ready-made communities — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/18/bumble-bffs-revamped-app-is-here-focusing-on-friend-groups-and-community-building).
- Bumble says 47% of young adults want more friends to do activities with — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/18/bumble-bffs-revamped-app-is-here-focusing-on-friend-groups-and-community-building).

**Clyx**
- Seeds supply by aggregating Ticketmaster and TikTok events.
- "Programs" are multi-session activities with the same group, built for repeated interaction.
- Miami beta in 2022, London in 2023. About 50k active ticket buyers and 200k browsers by late 2025.
- Sources: [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/); [TechCrunch, Sep 2025 [snippet]](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/10/this-gen-z-founded-app-just-raised-14m-to-take-on-the-loneliness-episdemic).

**222**
- Launched in Los Angeles with weekly events and a waitlist-style selection; local venues pay for foot traffic — [TechCrunch 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/14/2440702/).
- Later expanded to New York, Atlanta and other cities — [NexSpark [competitor]](https://nexspark.org/journal/222-review).

**Partiful**
- Growth came from link invites that work without an account, which makes every guest a potential host. It is used in 100+ countries (2025) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partiful).

**Les Amís**
- The Monday match drop creates a weekly ritual — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/).

### Inferences
- For a single-city launch such as Singapore, Timeleft's experience argues for **one fixed weekly slot** and **density over breadth**: about 50 weekly participants per market before splitting by neighbourhood.
- Repeat-group fatigue is real in small markets, so the grouping logic needs a rotation or freshness rule.
- Social proof ("people you know are going") was the strongest documented engagement lever (Pie, 2→10 minutes in app).

### Gaps
- No public retention cohorts (D30/D90) were found for any app.
- BFF Groups retention is not public — [NexSpark [competitor]](https://nexspark.org/journal/bumble-bff-review).
- No Asia/Singapore-specific launch data was found for Timeleft or the others. Wyzr Friends lists Singapore availability, but no traction data was found.

### Inferences
- Paid curation (Timeleft, 222, Les Amís) sells **certainty that a table will happen**. Its complaints focus on billing transparency and table composition (gender and age balance), so those are the trust-critical variables for any matched dining product.
- Free consumer apps monetize hosts (fees, ticketing, organizer subscriptions) or vanity features (Partiful Plus themes, Peek). A host-side model fits a free people-first dining feature, and a no-fee casual tier matches Meetup's 2025 "Starter" move.
- Paying to see who is attending (Partiful Peek) or to get priority (Meetup+) is controversial with communities; critics frame it as "pay to skip".

### Gaps
- Partiful Plus and Meetup+ prices are not disclosed in the primary sources retrieved.
- What each "Timeleft X" in-app purchase tier contains is not documented. They are probably 1-, 3- and 6-month plans, but this is unverified.
- App Store review themes for BFF (post-relaunch) were available only from competitor summaries; no independent review mining was done.
- Instagram has no researched native event or RSVP feature. Its role as baseline is limited to Facebook's Oct 2024 option to invite Instagram followers to Facebook events — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/04/facebook-launches-a-gen-z-focused-redesign).
- Nextdoor events specifics after the July 2025 redesign were not found.
