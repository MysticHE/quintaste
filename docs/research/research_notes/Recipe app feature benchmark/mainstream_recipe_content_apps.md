# Mainstream Recipe Content Apps: Feature Benchmark (status as of 17 October 2026)

Method note: App Store listings were fetched live on 17 Oct 2026, so their ratings, prices and version histories are current at that date. Apple shows recent entries without a year. Where the year is inferred (for example, NYT's "OS27 support", where iOS 27 is the 2026 release), the inference is stated. Google Play pages could not be fetched; Play figures come from third-party trackers and are marked as such. allrecipes.com and cooking.nytimes.com blocked fetching, so page composition for those comes from help-center text and secondary sources.

## 1. Which apps still operate in 2026, which have shut down, and at what scale?

### Takeaway
Three of the named apps are gone or have changed form. Yummly closed in December 2024. Food Network Kitchen closed on 1 May 2024. The Allrecipes recipe app (Dinner Spinner) was discontinued in March 2023 and replaced by a poorly rated magazine-reader app. NYT Cooking, Tasty, Kitchen Stories, Samsung Food, BBC Good Food, Epicurious and Cookpad are active in 2026, and NYT Cooking and Tasty have the largest rating volumes by far. SideChef's iOS app has not been updated since March 2025. ReciMe, a social-video import and organizer app, led US iOS Food & Drink top-grossing in March 2026.

## 2. What does each app's recipe detail screen contain, and in what order?

### Takeaway
No source documents a complete top-to-bottom order for any app. The fragments that are documented show a convergent pattern. A hero photo or video comes first, then title and rating, a short description, and action buttons (save, mark-cooked, share). Ingredients follow with scaling and unit controls, then steps, then nutrition, then personal notes, then community comments. NYT Cooking's documented placement is the most specific.

### Cited Findings
- **NYT Cooking (help center):**
  - "Mark as Cooked" sits below the recipe description, next to the star rating, in the app. On the web it is in the Ratings section.
  - "Nutritional Information" is below the ingredients and is described as a "rough estimate only".
  - Print and Share sit in an actions panel; printing can optionally include the photo. Shared recipes are gifted and readable for 30 days.
  - Metric and US units can be toggled, and recipes can be halved or doubled.
  - Ingredients can be shopped via Instacart — [NYT Help: Cooking Recipe Features](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/19686556670868-Cooking-Recipe-Features)
- **NYT Cooking notes placement:**
  - In the app, comments open from a message icon at the upper right of the recipe page.
  - "Your Private Notes", with an "Add a Note" button, sits **below the Preparation steps**.
  - On the web, comments are at the bottom of the page — [NYT Help: Recipe Comments and Private Cooking Notes](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/360011047512-New-York-Times-Cooking/19627732008468-recipe-comments-and-private-cooking-notes)
- **NYT Cooking (2023 UX critique):** the recipe page used an ingredients/preparation toggle. The preparation view omitted quantities, which forced users to switch back and forth. The critique recommended adding quantities into the steps — [Pratt IXD critique](https://ixd.prattsi.org/2023/02/design-critique-nyt-cooking-ios-app).
- **NYT Cooking 2026 recipe-page changes:**
  - v4.158.0 ("May 28", 2026): "New 'Cook Mode' in the recipe pages and ability to share photos to stories"
  - v4.170.0 ("Sep 22", 2026): "Cook Mode printing"
  - v4.160.0 (Jun 15): collection redesign
  - v4.166.0 (Jul 28): grouped filters — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nyt-cooking-recipes-tips/id911422904)
- **Epicurious (UX teardown):**
  - A prominent "Save Recipe" button gives immediate feedback.
  - A "Recipe Notes" section supports comment, tip and star rating, with likes and replies on comments.
  - Unsaving shows a toast with "Undo" — [ScreensDesign: Epicurious](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/epicurious).
  - The September 2024 v7.3.1 comment revamp added likes, replies, question/tip tags, multiple comments per recipe and custom usernames — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/epicurious/id312101965).
- **Samsung Food:**
  - Recipes saved from external sites show directions by opening the original blog page. The developer says "instructions are intentionally not imported, to honor the original creators."
  - v5.55.0 (Feb 2026) added multiple images per recipe.
  - v5.50.0 (26 Aug 2025) redesigned the tablet recipe page "optimized for landscape cooking."
  - Nutrition and a "health score" appear on 218,500+ recipes — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/samsung-food-meal-planner/id1133637674).
- **SideChef:** recipe cards show ingredient prices (redesign in v5.12.0, Dec 2021). The grocery page separates owned from needed ingredients (v5.15.0). The app shows how much of each ingredient a recipe uses — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sidechef/id905229928).
- **Allrecipes (web):** members post recipes, which staff copyedit. Members rate, review and add photos of finished dishes — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allrecipes).
  - Dotdash Meredith (now People Inc.) sister sites Food & Wine, Simply Recipes and EatingWell added a "Cook Mode" (distraction-free view that keeps the screen awake) by Sep 2025 — [Information Today, Sep 2025](https://www.infotoday.com/IT/sep25/Pack--Culinary-Websites-Have-Been-Cooking-Up-New-Features.shtml) (via search summary).
- **BBC Good Food:** shared recipe links open web pages that show "app-only content", and recipe text cannot be copied. The developer confirmed in-app printing is not possible (2024 reviews) — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/bbc-good-food/id533785308).

### Inferences
- The common above-the-fold set, inferred from the documented placements, is: hero media, title, rating, and a primary action row (Save/Cook Mode). Time and servings are likely near the fold. Nutrition, notes and comments are consistently below the steps.
- NYT places private notes directly below the steps, where users look after cooking. Comments sit behind an icon in the app. This keeps the main scroll focused on the recipe while still making notes discoverable.
- Moving the "Cook Mode" entry onto the recipe page itself (NYT 2026) is a recent pattern change worth benchmarking against.

### Gaps
- I found no source giving an exact element-by-element order or above-the-fold boundary for Tasty, Kitchen Stories, SideChef, BBC Good Food or Cookpad.
- Direct fetches of allrecipes.com and cooking.nytimes.com recipe pages were blocked, so web layout order was not verified first-hand.

## 3. What does each app's cook/step mode do?

### Takeaway
All active editorial apps now offer some cook mode, but they range from a simple "keep screen on" view (BBC Good Food, Dotdash/People Inc. sites) to paged step-by-step guidance with per-step media and timers (Kitchen Stories, SideChef, Tasty) and AI-generated step-by-step for any web recipe (Samsung Food). Voice control and smart-appliance control are mainly SideChef and Samsung features. I found little documentation of ingredient highlighting per step.

### Cited Findings
- **NYT Cooking:**
  - The listing describes an "always-on screen so recipes stay visible" — [App Store via search summary](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nyt-cooking-recipes-tips/id911422904).
  - A dedicated "Cook Mode" in recipe pages arrived in v4.158.0 ("May 28", 2026). Cook Mode printing followed in v4.170.0 (Sep 2026) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nyt-cooking-recipes-tips/id911422904).
  - An anecdotal forum report says NYT's iPad cooking mode puts ingredients and steps side by side and keeps the screen from dimming — [Straight Dope forum](https://boards.straightdope.com/t/modern-recipes-that-are-barely-recipes/998544/1) (anecdotal).
- **Tasty:**
  - The listing describes "video and step-by-step instructions, plus a cooking mode" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tasty/id1217456898).
  - Older release notes say the Step-By-Step mode keeps the phone awake while cooking — [APKMirror Tasty releases](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/buzzfeed/tasty/tasty-2-8-0-release/) (via search summary; exact version dates are inconsistent on the mirror).
- **Kitchen Stories:**
  - Cooking mode walks users through each step with photos and instructions — [Fueled "App of the Week"](https://fueled.com/blog/recipe-app).
  - Per-step timers and a serving-based measurement converter are mentioned in listings — [AppPricingLab listing](https://apppricinglab.com/app/google_play/com.ajnsnewmedia.kitchenstories) (via search summary).
- **SideChef:**
  - Each step has an image or video, plus built-in timers.
  - 2,000+ "CookAssist" recipes control LG, GE and Bosch Home Connect appliances (Thermador, Gaggenau).
  - Landscape mode on tablets since v5.19 (Nov 2022) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sidechef/id905229928).
  - Voice command is supported, along with in-app timers and preheating via Alexa Echo Show and Home Connect — [Home Connect partner page](https://www.home-connect.com/ae/en/works-with/food-recipes/sidechef).
  - The founder describes the concept as "GPS navigation" for cooking — [Forward Fooding](https://forwardfooding.com/?p=1323).
- **Samsung Food "Smart Cook Mode":**
  - v5.39.0 (20 Mar 2025) added an "AI-powered step-by-step Smart Cook Mode, including third-party sites."
  - v5.46.0 (2 Jul 2025) added servings and unit adjustment inside Smart Cook mode.
  - v5.48.0 (30 Jul 2025) added dynamic font sizes in guided cooking.
  - v5.49.0 (13 Aug 2025) made Smart Cook Mode the main button for planned recipes.
  - v5.53.0 (8 Oct 2025) blocked full-screen video ads from external sites inside Smart Cook Mode.
  - The listing places "Step-by-step Smart Cooking Mode" under Food+ (paid), which conflicts with release notes that present it as a general feature — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/samsung-food-meal-planner/id1133637674).
  - Appliance integration lets users set timers, preheat ovens and send cook settings to compatible Samsung appliances — [Samsung Newsroom 2023](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-announces-global-launch-of-samsung-food-an-ai-powered-personalized-food-and-recipe-service).
- **BBC Good Food:** "Cook Mode, which keeps the recipe on screen while cooking" is subscriber-gated within an all-subscriber app. Reviewers note that it prevents device sleep — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/bbc-good-food/id533785308).
- **Allrecipes family (People Inc.):** Food & Wine, Simply Recipes and EatingWell added a web Cook Mode that keeps the screen from dimming — [Information Today, Sep 2025](https://www.infotoday.com/IT/sep25/Pack--Culinary-Websites-Have-Been-Cooking-Up-New-Features.shtml) (via search summary).

### Inferences
- Wake lock is now baseline. Differentiation comes from per-step media, inline timers, scaling inside cook mode (Samsung), large or dynamic type (Samsung), landscape/tablet layouts (Samsung, SideChef) and printing (NYT).
- The 2023 critique of NYT's ingredients/steps toggle, which made users hold quantities in their heads, points to a benchmark: show step-specific quantities inside each step.

### Gaps
- I found no source confirming per-step ingredient highlighting, swipe paging versus scroll, or hands-free voice in NYT Cooking, Tasty, Kitchen Stories or BBC Good Food.
- I found no documentation of Tasty's step-mode video-per-step behavior in 2026.

## 4. How do apps handle servings scaling, unit conversion, substitutions and dietary filters?

### Takeaway
Scaling and metric/US conversion are standard; NYT offers only half or double, while Tasty, Kitchen Stories and Samsung allow arbitrary servings. Substitutions are emerging as an AI or paid feature: Samsung Food+ ingredient swaps and Tasty "Recipe Remixes". Diet, allergy and avoid-ingredient settings exist in Tasty, SideChef, Samsung Food (14 diets) and Cookpad.

### Cited Findings
- **NYT Cooking:** recipes can be halved or doubled, and units toggle between grams/liters and US standard. Search filters cover diet, cuisine and meal type, plus a "Highest Rated" (5-star) filter — [NYT Help](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/19686556670868-Cooking-Recipe-Features); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nyt-cooking-recipes-tips/id911422904). Grouped filters arrived in v4.166.0 (Jul 2026) and system dictation in search in v4.162.0 (Jun 2026) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nyt-cooking-recipes-tips/id911422904).
- **Tasty:**
  - Adjustable serving sizes with metric and US measurements.
  - Diet preferences, allergy settings and ingredient avoidance (added in v3.30).
  - "Ingredient search" generates dish ideas from three ingredients.
  - "Recipe Remixes" (v3.37, 5 Mar 2025) offer community variations, simpler or lower-calorie Tasty versions, and "Surprise Me!" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tasty/id1217456898)
- **Kitchen Stories:** a measurement converter adjusts quantities by servings — [AppPricingLab listing](https://apppricinglab.com/app/google_play/com.ajnsnewmedia.kitchenstories) (via search summary). An imperial-unit toggle existed in an older version, 4.0.3 — [Softpedia](https://mobile.softpedia.com/ios/Kitchen-Stories-Cookbook-iOS-Review-497.html). Ingredient-based (leftover) search is a Plus feature — [Kitchen Stories FAQ](https://www.kitchenstories.com/en/stories/faq-all-your-kitchen-stories-plus-questions-answered) (via search snippet; the page now returns HTTP 410).
- **Samsung Food:**
  - Free tier: filters for meal type, cook time, cuisine and **14 diets**, plus ingredient search across 42,000+ products in 26 languages.
  - Food+: "Recipe customization (servings, ingredient swaps, nutrition tweaks)".
  - Since v5.46.0, servings and units can be adjusted in Smart Cook mode — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/samsung-food-meal-planner/id1133637674)
- **SideChef:** filters by diet (vegan, vegetarian, keto, paleo), several allergens, preferences and "ingredients you have at home". "Zero Waste Grocer" (2024) suggests recipes for unused ingredients — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sidechef/id905229928). A 2026 third-party review mentions finding and swapping ingredients and sponsored products — [Tooliverse](https://tooliverse.ai/tools/sidechef).
- **Epicurious:** search by ingredient, diet, cuisine, cook time or rating — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/epicurious/id312101965). Filters cover meal type, dietary needs, cuisine, season and brand source (for example Bon Appétit) — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/epicurious).
- **Cookpad:** diet filters (vegan, keto, gluten-free) and appliance categories (air fryer, slow cooker). Automatic translation lets Spanish, Italian and Japanese recipes be read in English. Premium adds advanced filters — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cookpad-find-share-recipes/id585332633).
- **Food Network Kitchen (defunct):** the web fallback lost the profile-level diet and allergy settings — [Food Network help](https://help.foodnetwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/22105246548247-What-is-happening-to-the-Food-Network-Kitchen-app) (via search snippet).
- **NYT Cooking (substitutions):** a single 2019 App Store review praises the app's ingredient substitutions but notes that "recipes tend to underestimate prep and cooking time." I found no first-party documentation of a structured substitution feature — [App Store NYT review](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nyt-cooking-recipes-tips/id911422904).

### Inferences
- Benchmark tiers:
  - Basic: half or double plus a metric toggle (NYT).
  - Standard: free-form servings plus units (Tasty, Kitchen Stories).
  - Advanced: scaling inside cook mode plus AI substitutions (Samsung Food+, Tasty Remixes).
- Profile-level diet, allergy and avoid lists are treated as personalization infrastructure. Food Network explicitly lost them when it moved users to the web.

### Gaps
- I found no source on how substitutions are presented inline on the ingredient list (for example tap-to-swap versus a separate section) for any app.
- I found no source confirming whether NYT Cooking supports arbitrary servings beyond half and double as of 2026.

## 5. How do personalization, onboarding and discovery feeds work?

### Takeaway
Onboarding questionnaires feed "For You" or recommendation feeds (Kitchen Stories Plus, Epicurious, SideChef, Tasty). Discovery mixes editor-curated collections (NYT, Epicurious carousels) with AI assistants (Tasty Botatouille, Samsung AI meal plans) and pantry-based suggestions (Samsung Food+, Cookpad, SideChef). Epicurious forces sign-up and puts a paywall straight after onboarding.

### Cited Findings
- **Epicurious onboarding:** a 9-step flow with **mandatory sign-up** at the start, a screen showing the multi-brand catalogue, then a **paywall immediately after onboarding** (one-week trial converting to annual, "Save 33%"). The home screen uses curated carousels and a persistent search bar — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/epicurious). Daily recipe recommendations are based on preferences and diet — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/epicurious/id312101965).
- **Kitchen Stories Plus "For You" feed:** after a short questionnaire, users see recipes customized to their taste, nutritional requirements and in-app interactions. The regular feed stays available, and non-members get limited For You access — [Kitchen Stories: For You feed](https://www.kitchenstories.com/en/stories/the-for-you-feed-in-kitchen-stories-plus) (via search summary). Meal planning suggests lunch and dinner for the week filtered by diet and pantry, plus leftover-based recipes — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kitchen-stories-recipes/id771068291).
- **Tasty:** "AI Botatouille", an assistant powered by the ChatGPT API, suggests recipes based on preferences. Diet, allergy and avoid settings were added in v3.30. Content also comes from "Tasty Creators" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tasty/id1217456898).
- **NYT Cooking:** editor-curated collections, recipes from named cooks (Samin Nosrat, Ina Garten, Melissa Clark, Eric Kim), and new recipes and videos daily — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nyt-cooking-recipes-tips/id911422904). A 2023 critique says search suggestions look like recipes but open a single recipe, causing "information overload" — [Pratt IXD](https://ixd.prattsi.org/2023/02/design-critique-nyt-cooking-ios-app).
- **Samsung Food:**
  - **Free:** tailored meal plans for all users (apply a full-week plan up to 2 times free); "Try Next" removed from Home (v5.54.0, Jan 2026).
  - **Free:** nutrition goals with daily targets and macro breakdowns (v5.52.0, Sep 2025).
  - **Food+:** AI-personalized weekly plans, pantry management, nutrition tracking with adaptive plans — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/samsung-food-meal-planner/id1133637674)
  - Food+ at launch (IFA, Sep 2024) included photo-based Food List inventory, automatic logging of purchases, "Search with Your Food List" and expiry alerts. Free users got 3 days of meal-plan recommendations per week versus a full week for subscribers — [Samsung Newsroom IFA 2024](https://news.samsung.com/us/upgraded-samsung-food-raises-bar-for-food-experiences-ifa-2024/); [Stuff SA](https://stuff.co.za/2024/09/06/samsung-food-upgraded-ai-app-pay-for/).
- **SideChef:** personalization for discovery and meal planning was improved in v5.16.0 (Jun 2022). RecipeGen AI (Sep 2024) generates a shoppable recipe from a dish photo — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sidechef/id905229928).
- **Cookpad:** tell the app what is in the fridge to get dinner suggestions. Premium ranks "the most popular recipes at the top of search" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cookpad-find-share-recipes/id585332633).

### Inferences
- The industry is splitting into two kinds of personalization:
  - **Editorial curation**, as in NYT and Epicurious.
  - **Data-driven recommendations** built from pantry, nutrition-goal and preference data, as in Samsung, Kitchen Stories Plus and Tasty AI.
- Pantry and leftover features are often paywalled (Kitchen Stories Plus, Samsung Food+).
- Hard paywalls placed right after onboarding (Epicurious) contrast with NYT's metered or limited free access.

### Gaps

- I found no 2026 detail on how Tasty's Botatouille is surfaced in the UI.

## 6. How do saving/collections, shopping lists and meal planning work?

### Takeaway
Every active app has saving and collections. In 2025–2026 the big shift is **importing external recipes, including from Instagram and TikTok**: Kitchen Stories Plus, Cookpad, Samsung Food, SideChef and the grossing leader ReciMe. Shopping lists typically merge ingredients across recipes, and some push to retailers (NYT via Instacart; Tasty via Walmart; SideChef via Instacart, Walmart, Amazon Fresh and Target; Samsung via 23 retailers).

### Cited Findings
- **NYT Cooking:**
  - Recipe Box with personalized folders; "Mark as Cooked" saves into a Cooked Recipes list.
  - Built-in grocery list plus Instacart delivery (Instacart integration since June 2024).
  - A collection redesign (v4.160.0, Jun 2026) added descriptions, sorting and improved cards. iPad supports drag-and-drop folders — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nyt-cooking-recipes-tips/id911422904); [NYT Help](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/19686556670868-Cooking-Recipe-Features); [Information Today](https://infotoday.com/IT/sep25/Pack--Culinary-Websites-Have-Been-Cooking-Up-New-Features.shtml)
  - A 2023 critique says the grocery list was hidden inside the unlabeled Recipe Box — [Pratt IXD](https://ixd.prattsi.org/2023/02/design-critique-nyt-cooking-ios-app).
- **Tasty:** personalized cookbooks; shoppable recipes with Walmart delivery or pickup — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tasty/id1217456898).
- **Kitchen Stories:**
  - Custom cookbooks and collections, with imports from Instagram and TikTok via Plus.
  - Imported recipes can be edited before saving (v27.4.0, Aug 31).
  - Grocery lists can be viewed by recipe or combined — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kitchen-stories-recipes/id771068291)
- **Samsung Food:**
  - Save from any website.
  - Scan cookbook photos or handwritten recipes (v5.48.0, Jul 2025).
  - Recipe Builder with ingredient autocomplete (v5.53.0).
  - Weekly planner feeds the shopping list, with shared family lists, printable shared lists and reusable weekly plans. Planner notes are up to 400 characters.

- **SideChef:** meal plan by day, an auto shopping list, and checkout via Instacart, Walmart, Amazon Fresh and Target with real-time prices. Also My Pantry with a barcode scanner, and saving web recipes via Share or URL — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sidechef/id905229928). A 2026 review cites 1,300+ retailers — [Tooliverse](https://tooliverse.ai/tools/sidechef).

  - A weekly menu builds a shopping list that "add[s] only what's missing".

- **ReciMe:** shares Instagram or TikTok videos into the app to generate ingredients and steps; also offers grocery lists and a meal planner. Premium unlocks unlimited social imports and private uploads with a 7-day trial — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/recime-recipes-meal-planner/id1593779280). ### Inferences
- "Import anything, including social video" has become table stakes for 2026 recipe apps and a primary paid lever (unlimited-import caps in Kitchen Stories Plus, Cookpad Premium and ReciMe Premium).
- Shopping lists that subtract owned or pantry items (Cookpad "only what's missing", SideChef owned versus needed) are the differentiating detail.

## 7. What social and community features exist?

### Takeaway
NYT Cooking has the most structured comment system: public notes are moderated before they appear, upvoted notes surface as "Most Helpful", and private notes are subscriber-only. Cookpad's "Cooksnaps" (photos of cooked results) is the reference model for "I made this". Tasty and SideChef support photo and tip sharing. Epicurious revamped its comments in 2024 with likes, replies and tags.

### Cited Findings
- **NYT Cooking:**
  - Any registered NYT account, including non-subscribers, can post public comments under their name.
  - Comments cannot be edited and are pre-moderated: they appear only after staff approval.
  - Tabs: **All / Most Helpful** (sorted by upvotes via "Is this helpful?" or a thumbs-up) **/ Private**.
  - The in-app comments page has keyword search.
  - **Private notes are only for Cooking and All Access subscribers** and reappear in the same spot the next time the recipe is opened — [NYT Help](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/360011047512-New-York-Times-Cooking/19627732008468-recipe-comments-and-private-cooking-notes)
  - Star ratings and "Mark as Cooked" sit together — [NYT Help](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/19686556670868-Cooking-Recipe-Features).
  - "Share photos to stories" was added in v4.158.0 (2026) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nyt-cooking-recipes-tips/id911422904).
- **Cookpad:** "Cooksnaps" let users post photos of recipes they cooked with comments as community verification. Users can follow home cooks, keep recipes private, take part in "Challenges" and search for other cooks (2026 updates) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cookpad-find-share-recipes/id585332633).
- **Tasty:** a community for sharing photos and tips, ratings on every recipe, and community variations via "Recipe Remixes" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tasty/id1217456898). One 2020 reviewer alleged that negative tip comments are deleted (unverified) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tasty/id1217456898).
- **Epicurious:** v7.3.1 (Sep 2024) revamped comments with likes, replies, question/tip tags, multiple comments per recipe and custom usernames — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/epicurious/id312101965). Earlier (2019 and 2021) reviews said reviews could not be posted from the app — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/epicurious/id312101965).
- **SideChef:** rate recipes, upload photos, share tips — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sidechef/id905229928).
- **Samsung Food:** community notes and tips, public food communities, following friends and photo uploads (2022 review). The age rating cites messaging, chat and UGC — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/samsung-food-meal-planner/id1133637674).
- **BBC Good Food:** recipe commenting and rating plus "put questions to the expert team" are subscriber perks — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/bbc-good-food/id533785308); [AppFollow listing](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/bbc-good-food/533785308?country=nz) (via search summary).
- **Allrecipes (web):** ratings, reviews and photos of finished dishes; user-submitted recipes copyedited by staff — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allrecipes). People Inc.'s magazine copy cites "more than 60 million home cooks" — [OverDrive magazine listing](https://elibrarynj.overdrive.com/media/12444027) (marketing figure).

### Inferences
- Two models dominate:
  - **Curated public notes with helpfulness ranking** (NYT, Epicurious tags), which turns comments into recipe tips.
  - **Photo proof of cooking** (Cookpad Cooksnaps, Tasty, SideChef, Allrecipes photos).
- Gating private notes behind a subscription while leaving public comments free (NYT) is a deliberate monetization choice.

### Gaps
- I could not verify current Allrecipes "I Made It" or "Allstars" mechanics because the site blocked fetching and searches returned only spam.
- I found no 2026 data on whether Kitchen Stories still supports user comments or photos.

## 8. How is video used?

### Takeaway
Video ranges from Tasty's short overhead clips (the brand's core format) to NYT's longform shows, step-level clips in SideChef and Kitchen Stories, and expert masterclasses on paid tiers (BBC Good Food, SideChef Premium).

### Cited Findings
- **Tasty:** 10,000+ recipes with video. The Editors' Choice text cites more than 2,000 short step-by-step video recipes. A 2022 complaint says longer tutorials were replaced by faster "TikTok-style cuts" that make cooking stages harder to see — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tasty/id1217456898).
- **NYT Cooking:** step-by-step cooking videos, including longform shows; larger video playback on iPad — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nyt-cooking-recipes-tips/id911422904). Cooking guides and step videos are on NYT Cooking's YouTube channel — [NYT Help](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/19686556670868-Cooking-Recipe-Features).
- **SideChef:** images or video per step. Premium adds "Guided Recipe Videos from Culinary Experts" and on-demand classes — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sidechef/id905229928); [Home Connect](https://www.home-connect.com/ae/en/works-with/food-recipes/sidechef).
- **BBC Good Food:** step-by-step cooking videos and tutorials. Expert how-to videos and masterclasses are subscriber perks — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/bbc-good-food/id533785308).
- **Epicurious:** step-by-step cooking videos and instructional technique tutorials — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/epicurious/id312101965).

### Inferences
- Video length is a contested UX choice. Fast cuts drive social reach but frustrate learners (the Tasty complaint), which supports per-step clips over a single montage for cook mode.

## 9. What are the monetization models, and what do users praise or complain about?

- **Hard subscription:** BBC Good Food (app access subscriber-only) and Epicurious (recipes need a subscription or trial).
