Expo Go uses a browser handoff because its shared app scheme cannot support the usual production OAuth return. The app receives a launch link and a separate 256-bit proof. The browser has its own HttpOnly Secure SameSite=Lax cookie. After Google verifies the account, the browser shows the app code and explicit account confirmation. Close the browser and tap Finish Google sign-in within one minute. The proof atomically retrieves a single-use Better Auth token; the Expo client stores its signed session cookie in SecureStore. Proofs/launch/browser tokens are stored as hashes. The temporary handoff token is AES-256-GCM encrypted with a purpose-separated HKDF key and flow UUID as associated data. Start/finish/cancel are origin checked and rate limited. Expired flow records are swept on new flow creation. No Google password is collected.
