## Data and recovery boundaries

[Workspace schema](../src/state/recipe-workspace.ts) bounds each collection to 20 drafts, 40 ingredients and 30 steps per draft, 100 shopping notes, 300,000 characters per photo data URI and 1,200,000 aggregate recipe-photo URI characters. Photo input reuses the existing picker, which also enforces its stricter selected-file limit. JPEG/PNG/WebP data URIs are accepted; remote/SVG/oversized values are rejected. Preparation requires 1–8 servings, total time 1–1440 minutes, positive ingredient amounts with units and optional step timers of 1–180 whole minutes.

Guest/profile collections stay separate. Explicit guest copying deduplicates drafts/notes, retains the destination filter, clears copied publication consent and rejects capacity overflow before merge. Editing or deleting a draft does not publish it. Personal export omits recipe and step photo bytes; collection deletion clears drafts, shopping notes and other collection data. Local profile is not a real approved-creator account.
