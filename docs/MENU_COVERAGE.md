## In the app

Matched Explore cards have an independent dark Menu control with an authored open-menu icon. It retains a 48-unit target and does not open the place details or toggle Save. Place details retain their full Menu action.

The Menu screen offers dish search for long lists, immediate category selection and a source selector where multiple menus exist. Ordering/delivery sources appear before general brand or historical sources. Historical and uncertain source notices, currency uncertainty, starting prices, portions, schedules and dated availability qualifiers remain explicit. Source links keep their external-opening behavior.

**Correct** (accessible label: Correct menu) opens the existing correction flow. The Menu topic offers **Add**, **Edit** and **Remove**. Existing dishes can be selected by name, or an unlisted dish can be entered manually. Proposals retain original name/price, proposed dish name, optional SGD price/category, source, observed date and optional photo. A removal needs a reason. Incomplete prices can be saved in drafts and display as incomplete, rather than as a real amount.

Menu branch context survives topic changes and draft reloads. Structured changes survive owner-scoped storage, offline queuing, case receipts, additional-information states and reviewer inspection. Submitting a proposal does not change the public menu. The existing case service remains local-development-only; production identity, delivery and staffed review are separate outstanding work.
