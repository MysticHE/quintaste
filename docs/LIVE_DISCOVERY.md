## Retention and ownership

Google's Places policies permit storing place IDs indefinitely. The Places service terms allow Google-sourced latitude/longitude caching for up to **30 consecutive calendar days**, then require deletion. Other Google Places content has separate storage, display and attribution restrictions; the coordinate exception does not extend to names, addresses, phone, hours, status, ratings, reviews or photos. [Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies), [Maps service terms, section 14](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms/maps-service-terms).

| Data | Catalogue treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Google place ID | Permanent linkage key; preserve case. |
| Google latitude/longitude | If a cache is introduced, track expiry and delete within the 30-day limit. The current API cache is only five minutes and the collected directory has no coordinates. |
| Google business names, addresses, phone, hours/status, ratings/reviews and photos | Fetch/display as a separate live provider overlay under applicable rules and attribution. Do not turn them into permanent own-catalogue fields. The current preview requests these on demand for search/details and displays them with provider/author links. |
| Independently supplied merchant/contributor/editorial facts, ingredients and permitted photos | Can form QuinTaste's permanent catalogue when provenance, evidence, dates and permission/consent are retained. |
| User's private notes and lists | Device-owned private collection data; they do not confirm public dietary evidence. |

Copying a Google field, relabelling its source, transcribing a Google listing or downloading a Google photo does not make that data independently sourced. Keep provider metadata separate from facts received directly from a merchant, contributor or original editorial work.

## How to grow a permanent QuinTaste catalogue

This is the proposed architecture; the merchant-owned catalogue, intake, moderation and publication services are **not implemented**.

1. Keep Google IDs as durable links to businesses and deduplicate branches by ID. Treat search matches as candidates until reviewed.
2. Obtain facts directly: merchant-supplied branch/contact details, ingredient and preparation statements, original contributor observations, or editorial research with a permitted source. Obtain permission for photos and contributor/merchant submissions.
3. Store QuinTaste facts separately from the live Google overlay. Each fact needs a source/author, evidence or source document, observed/reviewed date, scope (branch/dish/variation), permission/consent and withdrawal/staleness state. Record ingredient uncertainty and cross-contact separately.
4. Publish confirmed/no-allium filtering only after review rules accept relevant evidence. A private answer or unsent correction draft does not become public confirmation automatically.
5. Refresh the Google overlay on demand and enforce field-specific retention/attribution. A future coordinate cache needs an expiry/deletion process; it must not silently become the permanent location source.

The resulting catalogue can grow from independent evidence while Google provides current business discovery and linkage. Real accounts, cloud sync, staffed moderation, merchant editing and production API hosting are still separate work.
