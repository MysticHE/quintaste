STORY: Completed profile on an age-checked account and explicit visibility > invitation > recipient accepts > pre-meal chat > propose a public place, future Singapore time and food arrangements > full group and individual confirmations. Edits or membership changes reset confirmations. Pause stops new invitations and preserves chat. Block closes affected groups; unblock never restores them. Clear messages, delete closed groups with message cleanup and delete unsent reports while keeping blocks. Old booking/check-in/Kaki routes redirect; old records remain private/exportable.

Home applications collect neighbourhood, not an exact home address or identity documents.
